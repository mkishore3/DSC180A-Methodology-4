**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic covered in my domain this quarter has been the non-prompt based perturbations. For example, one case we read about was how being signed in or having a paid account for ChatGPT resulted in differences in responses from the model. I think this reveals that there are nuances to bias in LLMs that may not be immediately obvious. It adds an additional layer to our project that we must be aware of in experimentation.

A potential investigation I would like to pursue for our Quarter 2 project is exploring how gender bias may exist in models, specifically for career suggestions in tech. I think it will be interesting to see if users with the same experience level, background, and skill set may be led towards a managerial tech role or a technical tech role. We would use a list of intersectional names with labeled race and gender. I think in Quarter 2 I'd want to add more perturbations to the prompt itself like in skillset and years of experience.
